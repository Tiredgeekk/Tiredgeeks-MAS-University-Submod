## 📚 Tiredgeeks MAS University Discussion Submod 📚

"University can be exhausting, but at least we can talk about it together, right, player?" ~ Monika

A collection of new conversation topics where Monika chats with you about university life — from classes and studying to professors, assignments, exams, group projects, majors, and all the little experiences that come with being a student.

## 📚 Features

New conversation topics focused on university and student life.

Interactive dialogues where Monika can remember your year, major, and minor.

Topics about exams, assignments, studying, procrastination, professors, group projects, and more.

New "I Feel" topics for talking to Monika about things like burnout, grades, academic stress, and other difficult feelings.

University-themed goodbyes and "be right back" topics for when you need to head to class, study, grab lunch, or take a break.

A little encouragement from Monika for those days when university feels like it's trying to consume your entire existence.

## 📚 Installation Instructions

Download the submod by pressing code, and then download zip.

Make sure you have the latest version of Monika After Story installed.

Open your MAS game folder, then go into the Submods folder.

Drop the University Discussion submod folder inside.

Launch the game — your new university topics will appear during conversation!
